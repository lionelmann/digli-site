Questions, answered.

How do I scan a record?

Tap the gold camera button. Point at an album cover or a barcode and hold steady. Digli identifies the release and drops it in your Dig Pile. You can also add a record by hand with Look Up Record.

What are the 10 free scans?

New collectors get 10 scans with no card. After that, scanning pauses until you subscribe to Digli Pro. Your Shelf, Dig Pile, and Want List stay on the phone either way.

Where did my scan go?

Every scan lands in the Dig Pile first — that’s the inbox. Move keepers to Your Shelf (owned) or bookmark them on the Want List (still hunting).

What is Dig Profile?

Dig Profile (the collector portrait in Taste) is built from records you file on Your Shelf. Personality, hidden insight, DNA, and eras fill in as the shelf grows. The full portrait is part of Digli Pro.

What does Collection Match mean?

Collection Match scores how a record fits what you already own — and lists why (shared DNA, eras, textures). Use it in the shop before you buy. Worth the Hunt does the same for records you don’t own yet.

The camera won’t open

iPhone Settings → Digli → Camera should be on. If you still see “Camera view is not available,” force-quit the app and try again. If the cover isn’t recognized, try a flatter photo or the barcode.

How does Discogs import work?

Open Discogs in the app and import with your username or by connecting the account. Free includes one import. Digli Pro unlocks ongoing sync so the shelf stays current.

How do I cancel Digli Pro?

Apple handles the subscription. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Digli → Cancel. You’ll keep Pro until the end of the paid month.

I paid but the app still looks free

Open the subscription screen in Digli and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with. Still stuck? Send a note from Feedback.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your Digli account in the app. Delete account — this removes your Digli data. Apple subscriptions are cancelled separately in Apple ID settings.
